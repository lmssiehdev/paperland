Paper.io 2 Teams Mode

Paper.io 2 Teams takes the experience of the world’s most beloved multiplayer .io game to a whole new level. Now you can capture territory, defend your land and attack other players together with a team of comrades from around the world! The battles take place on a giant paper circle representing a globe, so the stakes are higher than ever. Can you paint the world into your color, outsmart the enemies and dominate the server?

All your favorite mechanics are still present: draw a line by moving your character around to add a piece of paper to your territory – it will be automatically captured and painted into your color, as soon as the line is finished. The enemies will try to do the same, but you can counter-attack by breaking their lines before they are completed – doing so will also destroy the unlucky opponent. Be careful not to fall into the same trap: always try to stay close to your paper lands and cooperate with your teammates by adding smaller chunks of territory at a time.

It’s easy to track your team’s progress by looking at the chart on the bottom right that states the percentage of territories occupied by the players. To assure your victory, work together with your comrades, eliminate rivals and capture 100% of the paper globe. See you in Paper.io 2 Teams online battles!
