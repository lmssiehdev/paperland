Battle Royale Paper.io 2

The new Paper.io 2 Battle Royale mode is about to blow the fans away. Inspired by the titans of the genre, it enhances the familiar IO mechanics. Fight for territory on a circular arena by outlining the borders with a colored trail. Conquer as much as possible and mind other players who will try to attack. This time there is an additional challenge to consider. The playable area shrinks periodically leaving less space for everyone to occupy. This forces the competitors to engage in combat and makes the rounds even more intense. Master this engaging playstyle and get an advantage over others early on. Achieve one victory after another and become the undefeated champion of the server.

Control the marker by moving the mouse cursor or pressing WASD. Alternatively, use the Arrow keys for the same result. The safe zone is indicated by the green dotted line. Stay inside to avoid getting squished by the borders. Once in a while, they will move inwards pushing the combatants towards the center. Keep adding to the existing territory. Draw around untaken and already occupied lands to annex them. Be careful not to let a rival crash into the trail before returning to base. If they are successful, this will mean instant death. Use this technique to get rid of the competition. The last player to survive earns the top prize.

It was only a matter of time before this match made in heaven was developed. Implementing time-tested designs once introduced by the likes of PUBG never fails to impress. Now everyone can enjoy this new addition to the franchise for free. Paper.io Battle Royale online sessions only last for a few minutes. However, they usually turn out to be the most memorable competitive experiences ever. Feel the pure adrenaline of these multiplayer matches coursing through the veins.
